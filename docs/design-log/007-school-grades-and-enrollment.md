# School grades and enrollment (3 November 2026)

Validation: all **436 tests** pass on Node 24.14.0 after `npm ci`, including simulation, migration, and DOM integration checks. All **24 local HTTP assets** return 200 and match source. The 4,000-life percentile reference was regenerated without fitting and reproduces byte-for-byte; the independent 1,600-life cohort passes. Browser/visual checks remain omitted by preference.

These rules supersede the earlier generic Sports/Extracurricular focuses and school budget.

School years (ages 5-17) have a ten-point base budget, adjusted by birth conditions. School, Friends, Family, and Yourself use 0-4 points with default/neutral 2. Sports and Extracurriculars each use 0-2 with default/neutral 1 after enrollment; each stays locked at 0 while unenrolled. Joining requires a free point. Leaving frees its points, while keeping an activity preserves its success. Switching starts success at 5.0 again. College/adult and toddler rules are unchanged.

School GPA shows **N/A** at age 5 until the first school year is completed. Afterward, the Year screen shows the latest report-card letter, from A+ through F-. Study points 0-4 set baseline scores of 50, 65, 80, 90, and 97. Intelligence adjusts that baseline by `2 * (clamp(effectiveIntelligence, 0, 10) - 5)`, a bounded -10 to +10 adjustment; the final score is clamped to 50-100 and rounded to one decimal. Study effort drives the grade without multiplying by Intelligence. Intelligence 8 with two study points and Intelligence 3 with three both earn 86 (B); even Intelligence 0 can earn 87 (B+) with four study points. Saved GPA and historical report cards retain their recorded values, while future years use this scale. Schema 19 is unchanged; historical grade validation checks stored bounds rather than recomputing past results under current balance rules. Each completed school year uses its starting Intelligence and study allocation; the report card records both inputs and the previous/new grade. Use `reportedAge: null` to display N/A and No report card yet; keep the numeric initialization field for save compatibility without displaying it as an earned grade. The first completed report has `previousGpa: null`, and its earned grade appears at age 6. Existing age-five saves also show N/A without rewriting saved ratings or RNG; already completed reports retain their recorded grades. Imported school records with no completed report follow the same rule. Letter lower bounds are A+ 97, A 93, A- 90, B+ 87, B 83, B- 80, C+ 77, C 73, C- 70, D+ 67, D 63, D- 60, F+ 57, F 53, F- 50. College admissions are unchanged.

There are 20 sports and 22 extracurriculars in `school.js`. Each category presents five saved random offers annually, excluding last year's five and the current selection. The current activity can be retained even when absent from the list. Offers use a local deterministic shuffle; opening menus, forecasts, reloads, and migration do not consume gameplay RNG. Lacrosse requires Lifestyle 7.0 or higher; losing eligibility pauses participation and caps its focus at 0, retaining enrollment and success. The player must restore focus after becoming eligible again.

Basketball/volleyball performance uses Strength and same-age height; soccer/football/tennis use Strength; cheerleading uses Strength and Attractiveness. Fencing also uses Intelligence, gymnastics Creativity. All sports train Strength and Social; fencing additionally trains Intelligence and gymnastics Creativity. Chess trains only Intelligence, instruments only Creativity, and modeling only Attractiveness. Other clubs train their explicitly listed stats, with some adding Social. Sports no longer gives Health, and extracurricular effects follow the enrolled activity. No enrollment or ineligible enrollment gives no activity training effect.

Activity success starts at 5.0/10 and changes annually by `0.35 * (weightedRelevantStats - 5) + 0.6 * (focus - 1) + 0.12 * boundedNormalDraw`, rounded to hundredths and clamped to 0-10; display uses one decimal. Every eligible enrollment takes one separate normal draw; forecasts use zero noise and preserve RNG. Height uses an age-relative reference, without introducing an adult-height target. Training uses one negative unit at 0, neutral at 1, and one positive unit at 2, shared with inline signs. Per-unit inputs are Intelligence/Attractiveness .225, Creativity/Social .24, Strength .3 before yearly scale, aptitude, age response, directional damping, and bounds. Modeling combines with school Yourself appearance input before response.

Core school effects remain: School Intelligence -3,-2,0,+2,+3 units and Creativity -2,-1,0,+1,+2; School reduces Social and mental stability. Friends affects Social and mental stability without Creativity. Yourself affects Attractiveness, Health, and mental stability. Family retains its existing effects.

Excellent grades (90+) can trigger celebration and parental pride; poor grades (below 70) can trigger difficult conversations and grounding. Four concrete household events affect parent mental stability/relationships and personal wellbeing/Social; grounding also caps Friends at 1 for one complete playable turn. They use the same starting-year grade as the report and share the five-event annual maximum. Existing personal report-card events use matching grade gates. The catalog now contains 309 event definitions and 73 decisions.

Schema 19 adds a school record, preserving existing ratings, RNG, effects, pending decisions, and history. Old school saves gain a deterministic starting grade, five offers per category, and no enrollment; former generic Sports/Extracurricular points become 0. Only active allocations are fitted to the ten-point budget/limits. At age 5, both optional categories start locked; no crafts points transfer into enrollment. At 18, the final school record remains and school focuses disappear. Succession starts a fresh school record for a school-age heir. New summaries store per-category maxima of 2; historical four-key or six-key reviews retain their original points, maxima, and budgets. Physical restrictions from the old Yourself scale translate to Sports: self caps 0/1 -> Sports 0, 2/3 -> Sports 1, 4/5 are inert; explicit Sports caps apply directly. The save key remains `lifelines.save.v1`.
