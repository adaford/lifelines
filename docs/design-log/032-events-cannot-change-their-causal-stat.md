# Events cannot change their own causal stat (12 November 2026)

A stat-driven event cannot directly change the same person's triggering stat, in either direction. This applies to every automatic outcome and every choice, including family relationship factors, explicit stat thresholds, school-grade Intelligence inputs and the special abandonment formula. Treat each person separately: Dad's Intelligence may affect the player's Intelligence, but cannot change Dad's Intelligence. Household Lifestyle is shared. Target-affinity bounds are safeguards rather than event causes. Determine causes from the event rules, never from optional displayed cause text.

Remove 79 conflicting catalog contributions across 67 workspace definitions and 65 across 53 release definitions. These include ten direct Fame changes: fame-only event rates depend on the Fame rating, so their other effects remain while Fame gains/losses come from selected focus. Preserve fame initiation, ongoing +5 Social learning ability, activity training, support and the chance of losing fame. Casual encounters no longer give a Social reward because Social affects their odds. Accepted/rejected proposals no longer add/subtract relationship because that relationship affects acceptance. Keep the odds, random draws, status changes and pregnancy rules unchanged.

Abandonment still changes care arrangements and can affect the player or remaining caregiver, but does not reduce the departing parent's causal relationship rating. Preserve all other event fields, choices, IDs, eligibility, probabilities, independent rolls and the five-event maximum. Some choices/events now have no direct rating change; do not invent replacement effects. Keep all 385 workspace / 350 release definitions and 79 decisions. Source-effect invariants cover the complete catalog, choices, owner identity, explicit gates, Fame and rare cross-stat affinity events.

Apply the new rules only when future outcomes resolve. Preserve current ratings, saved history/reviews, old temporary effects, pending choice IDs, focus and RNG on load; schema remains 20. Recompute the observed percentile references without fitting or compensating for the changed consequences. Cache token: causal-stat-outcomes. Browser/visual checks remain omitted by preference.
