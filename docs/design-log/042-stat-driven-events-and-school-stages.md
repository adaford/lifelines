# Stat-driven events, household events, low-health deaths and school stages (24 November 2026)

Balance numbers now live in one module, `balance.js` (`EVENT_BALANCE`, `MORTALITY_BALANCE`, `BIRTH_BALANCE`, `FOCUS_BALANCE`, `SCHOOL_BALANCE`). Other modules only read them.

**Events are mostly caused by ratings.** One curve, `statEventFactor`, scales an event by how far its causing rating sits from 5 in the event's direction: 0.4 at 5, rising to 4 at the extreme (`factorCurve` sets the shape; below 1 it rises like a logarithm), fading to 0 toward the opposite extreme. Both low and high ratings now have a pool floor (`statPoolTarget`): starting one point from neutral, the chance that at least one event caused by that rating happens reaches 90% at 0 (low) or 60% at 10 (high), with no single event allowed more than 30% of its pool, so a pool is shared among many events. A relative's ratings drive half as much. Retired events no longer take a share. Events that no rating, relationship or grade causes are "random" (focus-driven incidents included): their expected volume is split evenly between good and bad outcomes and capped at one random event per three expected stat-driven events (`statToRandomRatio`). An event that happened last year is 0.15 times as likely (`repeatFactor`). Life-stage "support" events with an Attractiveness source are now caused by high, not low, Attractiveness. `scripts/event-matrix.mjs` prints every live event by cause and effect. In 150 seeded lives at default focus: about 1.1 events a year in childhood and 2.7-3.5 as an adult, about 80% stat-driven, random events split evenly, and the same event recurs the next year for under 1% of occurrences (was 13.7%).

**Household events.** An event marked `household` changes everyone at home: every caregiver while the player is a minor, or the partner and minor children for an adult. The night road works, building-repair noise and construction noise events are household events, roll once per household, and have shorter stories.

**Choices are trade-offs.** In 23 choice events one option was at least as good as the other on every effect (for example a benefit against nothing). Each option now carries its own benefit, never the event's causing stat (032). `test/choice-tradeoffs.test.js` keeps every live choice event that way.

**Low Health can kill at any age.** On top of the age-based odds, Health below 4 adds a yearly death chance, everyone included: 5% at Health 2, 10% at 1, 20% at 0, doubling per point lost and fading out between 2 and 4 (`MORTALITY_BALANCE`). It counts as disease. Game rule, not a medical estimate.

**Ratings at birth** are close to a normal curve around 5 with a standard deviation of about 1 (first generation: noise 0.8 and 0.3 per affinity step; inheritance: parents' mean plus a spread of 1), so a newborn below 2 is very rare.

**Focus.** School years share 9 points (was 10). Friends raises only Social. Family (and toddler Family time) changes none of your own ratings: it supports each caregiver's Health, Mental stability and relationship. Yourself still carries your Mental stability. Crying also strains or eases caregivers' Health. Parents' Health is shown on the main screen and in the year review.

**School.** Sports and extracurriculars are always offered the year school starts. Activities train exactly the stats they are graded on (height excepted), each trained stat getting its unit divided by the number of trained stats; no sport trains Social any more, but zero points or no sport still costs Strength and Social. Elementary (5-10), middle (11-13) and high school (14-17) each keep a cumulative GPA that starts fresh with the stage, shown on the Career tab during school; only the high school GPA counts for college. Finishing elementary or middle school with B+ (87) or better offers three random stats to choose from for +0.75; below C (73) the parents shout and one of Social, Creativity or Mental stability falls by 0.5. Neither touches Intelligence (032). These milestones are scheduled, not rolled, and sit outside the five-event limit; their options use a local seeded stream, not the gameplay RNG.

**Display.** Timed lines read "-2 Play focus this year" or "... for 2 years" instead of a separate "Temporary effects" line. Cause notes ("Low health contributed to this event.") are bold italic in their own color. The parents panel aligns its three bars. The sport and extracurricular chooser no longer opens with explanation paragraphs.
