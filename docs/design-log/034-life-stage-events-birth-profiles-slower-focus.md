# Life-stage event coverage, realistic birth profiles, and slower focus changes (13 November 2026)

Added 688 ordinary experiences: 112 personal and 576 family definitions, including siblings. The catalog now contains 1,073 definitions and 79 decisions. Seven personal ratings plus shared Lifestyle each cause an event affecting every other rating; family coverage also includes Relationship. Every pair has both a benefit and a setback, with age-specific stories for infancy, toddlerhood, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, midlife and later life where the relationship exists. Parents and guardians share the adult parent pathway. Height remains a growth measurement and Fame retains its separate earned-status rules; there is no separately simulated friend actor.

Stories use explicit causal mechanisms, age-appropriate activities and caregiver arrangements. Mental-stability consequences require sustained strain or dependable support. New events affect one other stat, never their own causal stat. All 385 earlier definitions and all existing choices are preserved. Signed catalog entries are 535 positive and 489 negative (52.25% / 47.75%); 49 are neutral. This balances the catalog, not a guarantee that each life receives equal good and bad outcomes. Supplemental additions retain small trait-sensitive independent odds without multiplying the existing low-stat hazard floors. The shared five-event annual limit remains.

Practical household access changes last one complete year. They are saved separately from legacy Lifestyle effects, apply once to the relevant household, and expire without changing underlying caregiver career support or passing to an heir. Stories are captured at occurrence using the subject's starting age. Rendering and reloading never reinterpret saved stories.

New births draw from 48 profiles: 22 helpful, 22 challenging and four mixed. All 100 legacy perk IDs and their saved mechanics remain valid. Profiles distinguish autism's individual support needs and strengths, specific learning disabilities without a general Intelligence penalty, exceptional reasoning, and bipolar predisposition rather than a diagnosis at birth. These are simplified fictional traits, not diagnostic criteria, prevalence estimates, or claims of single-gene inheritance. Clinical framing follows [NIMH autism](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/autism-spectrum-disorder), [NICHD learning disabilities](https://www.nichd.nih.gov/health/topics/learning/conditioninfo), and [NIMH bipolar disorder](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/bipolar-disorder).

Halved raw annual focus-driven gains and losses, including player activity training/inactivity, autonomous relative practice, family care, relationships and Fame maintenance. Infant crying, event amounts, aging, financial formulas and activity success grades retain their own rules. Aptitude, directional response, rounding and bounds still apply, so final displayed deltas need not be exactly half after other causes combine. Preserve saved ratings, focus, aptitudes, history, pending choices, effects and RNG on load; schema remains 20. Recompute observed percentiles without fitting. Cache token: life-realism. Browser/visual checks remain omitted by preference.

Audit: `node scripts/audit-events.mjs --baseline .git/life-realism-baseline/events.json --output test-artifacts/life-events-audit.json` checks 3,664 stage/relationship/source/target/sign cells with no missing coverage or causal conflicts. The baseline comparison confirms every previous definition is unchanged. The portable audit command also works without the optional local baseline. Changes remain local; no release or publication was requested.

Validation: all 642 workspace tests pass, including the independent 1,600-life cohort, actual sibling/birthday rolls, orphan Fame support, legacy effects, reloads and one-year expiry. The 4,000-life observed reference reproduces byte-for-byte without fitting. 5,094 differential checks confirm focus scaling; all 32 local HTTP assets match source and the temporary server is stopped.
