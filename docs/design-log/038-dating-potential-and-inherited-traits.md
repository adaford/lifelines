# Dating potential and inherited traits (14 November 2026)

From age 18, Year and Family show Your dating potential, including while partnered. The derived 0-10 weighted mean gives height, Attractiveness and Lifestyle one share each; Intelligence, Strength, Social, Creativity, Health and Mental stability each get .2 shares. Height uses the adult reference for the person's saved growth profile. The same pure score drives yearly encounter slots/chances, typical prospect ratings and mutual-interest odds. Social focus, housing, existing relationships, adulthood and retirement gates still apply. Up to five prospects can arrive; quantity and quality remain random. Default male players meet women, and saved gender preferences remain supported.

Dating profiles show all seven personal ratings with their learning affinities, plus Lifestyle, height and perks. The player's potential panel is separate from the prospect. Rendering, help and reloads never reroll prospects, change ratings, add saved fields or consume RNG.

Pure genetics.js supplies parental inheritance for every newly generated biological newborn, including a new player. Each of the seven base ratings and each of the seven affinities centers on the two biological parents equally, with separate seeded variation. Saved base ratings are used rather than temporary effective bonuses. A captured pregnancy retains its other biological parent after a breakup. Newborn perks apply their affinity shifts once afterward. Adoption and unrelated heirs retain the independent population generator; household Lifestyle remains environmental support.

GENETICS_BALANCE.randomness = 1 is the shared tuning control: zero removes the random deviation from inherited rating, affinity and growth-tendency means, and larger values broaden it. Default normal deviations are ratingDeviation = 2.5, affinityDeviation = 1.6 and growthDeviation = .047, using the existing bounded normal draw. Ratings remain 0-10, affinities remain integer -3 to 3, and growth tendencies remain .8-1.2. High-rated/high-affinity parents generally have higher-rated/higher-affinity children, but opposite outcomes remain possible. Perk rolls and later development retain their separate randomness.

Height inheritance normalizes each mature parent's attained height using their own growth reference and passes on a growth tendency with variation. There is no predetermined adult-height destination or catch-up; age, Health and later seeded growth still matter. Existing heights and growth records are preserved.

Active perks gain 25% relative odds per carrying biological parent (50% for both), controlled by INHERITANCE_CHANCE_BOOST_PER_PARENT = .25. Equivalent legacy profiles boost their current successors. The 57 legacy traits without a suitable successor have only a rare inheritance route, at LEGACY_INHERITANCE_CHANCE_PER_PARENT = .003 per carrying parent; independent births never draw them. All 148 known parental IDs have a route, with no duplicate parent counting and the existing five-perk/group limits. These are fictional game settings, not genetic probabilities or medical claims.
