# Mental-stability event variety (11 November 2026)

Add 24 automatic events: 12 for the player's mental stability and 12 for parents or the current guardian during childhood. Each set has four high-stability opportunities, five low-stability disruptions, and three support/recovery events. The catalog now contains 346 definitions and 73 decisions. Preserve all existing event IDs, probabilities, choices, recorded outcomes, and the shared five-event annual maximum.

New catalog-only `minStability`/`maxStability` gates use the player's starting effective mental stability or the named parent's starting rating. Low events unlock at 4 or below, moderate disruptions at 3 or below, and major crises at 2 or below. High opportunities start at 7, with major high-stability opportunities at 8. Existing curved stat factors make eligible events more likely toward the corresponding extreme. Major events have lower base chances and substantially larger effects than everyday setbacks. These thresholds and probabilities are fictional gameplay rules.

Examples include canceled outings, missed school meetings, mediation, school advocacy, reliable support networks, counseling places, and practical recovery support. A parent's psychiatric admission adds base +1.2 to their mental stability but costs the player -1 mental stability and -.65 Social, with -1 School and Friends capacity for one complete year. Severe care disruption costs the player -1.1 mental stability, -.7 Social, and -.25 Health. The player's involuntary psychiatric admission (age 13+) costs -1.1 Social through separation while treatment adds +.8 mental stability; it gives -2 School/Career and Friends capacity and +2 Yourself minimum for one complete year. All rating amounts are permanent raw changes before the shared directional response and bounds. Receiving care does not itself reduce the parent-child relationship.

Reviews, journal entries, and odds label new events Minor, Moderate, or Major. New event reviews retain the full explanation, including both disruption and recovery; existing historical prose is untouched. Mental-stability and parent help explain the threshold and recovery rules. No save migration or schema change: preserve ratings, focus, effects, histories, pending decisions, and RNG on load.
