# Current focus rules (1 November 2026; supersede earlier focus values)

- Every player focus uses integer 0-4 with default/neutral 2. Base budget is 2 times active areas: toddler 10, school/college/adult 8, adjusted by birth conditions. All personal and caregiver choice stat/relationship contributions are neutral at 2.
- Ordinary effects use shared `focusEffect` values -3,-1,0,+1,+3 for allocations 0,1,2,3,4: endpoints are triple the adjacent deviation before aptitude, age, bounds, and rounding. Signs are `---`, `-`, omitted, `+`, `+++`. Arts & crafts Intelligence is the sole exception: use -1,0,0,0,+1 raw contribution steps with coefficient .27 (including the sixfold choice multiplier), showing one minus only at 0 and one plus only at 4. Crafts Creativity keeps the ordinary curve. Share this exception between actual effects and previews. Combined adult totals retain support for six signs. Electronics Social reverses direction. Do not restore the older half-paced crafts rule or 3-point neutral exceptions.
- Unspent points have no separate stat/rest reward. Adult combined previews isolate focus contributions rather than background development; default allocations show no focus effects. Keep infant crying and natural background development separate.
- The single-unit correction retains schema 17 without changing saved ratings, allocations, history, or RNG on load. Schema 17 gives old people empty condition lists and migrates only active allocations to new caps/budgets deterministically; keep stats, effects, RNG, history and pending choices intact. Historical summaries retain old points/max/budget. New summaries store focusMax and focusBudget. Saved caps at or above 4 are inert. Transitioning to school enforces its reduced budget.
- Autonomous parent career focus retains its internal 0-5 model. Translate player focus to the previous non-stat scale for career/education progress, finances and encounter/event odds; default 2 keeps prior neutral pacing.
