# Career potential and logarithmic Lifestyle (13 November 2026)

All 100 existing job IDs, titles, qualifications and pay anchors remain, with Computer scientist added as the 101st role. Reviewed success factors now emphasize the abilities relevant to each role: Sales uses Social and Intelligence equally; appearance is confined to visual presentation roles; physical Strength is retained where the work demands it. Eleven existing fit maps changed. Each job has an authored advancement path, a potential rating and an internal earning ceiling. Employed trades include specialist and supervisory progression; business ownership is a separate higher-upside role. These are fictional game settings, informed by the BLS occupation references in career-profiles.js, not real salary limits.

Pure career-rules.js shares weighted skill fit, focus, experience, advancement and capped pay across player and caregiver careers. CAREER_BALANCE.advancementRate = .08 is the main progression control. No career-focus allocation means no automatic advancement. Career progress resets when changing or leaving a role; inheritance and reserves can support Lifestyle beyond a particular job's earning ceiling. Career cards and current roles show success factors, earned progress, an accessible Advancement potential bar, and career Lifestyle ceilings. Saved caregiver occupations display the same role potential.

Pure lifestyle.js supplies an invertible logarithmic resource scale and directional Lifestyle contributions that slow toward both poles. LIFESTYLE_BALANCE.changeScale = 1 controls direct contribution size; edgeExponent = 1.5 controls resistance, and reserveDrawRate = .04 sets fictional reserve support. The final 9.9-to-10 step represents ten times the supporting resources. Four-decimal internal precision and a dedicated display rule prevent sub-10 values appearing as 10.0. Family costs, Fame and new household support use the directional rule; saved legacy Lifestyle modifiers remain exact additive effects until expiry.

Schema stays 20. Optional careerProgress, economyVersion and caregiver jobId/advancement fields are validated without adding them to loaded saves. Old ratings, job tenure, offers, focus, history, effects, pending decisions and RNG remain unchanged on load. The new economy activates on the next complete annual routine. Old parent occupations are not invented during a read. Career and Lifestyle constants are shared with pure forecasts; no extra gameplay random draws were introduced.

Independent checks cover 24 genuine pre-change saves and 409 preservation/transition assertions. Career tests cover all 101 caps, 120 actual career years, switching, household wealth, pole precision, zero-input stability and reloads.
