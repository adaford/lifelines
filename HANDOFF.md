## Birth conditions

New players, parents, guardians, children, dating prospects, and unrelated heirs receive zero to five distinct conditions. Either biological parent having a condition multiplies its roll chance by 1.5; both parents do not compound the boost. Adoption uses independent rolls. Candidates are shuffled before the five-condition cap, and mutually exclusive catalog groups cannot occur together. These are fictional game mechanics.

Condition stat changes apply once at creation, rounded to two decimals and bounded 0-10. Point adjustments and stage-specific caps last throughout life. Conditions persist through aging, relationships, reloads, and succession without repeating birth bonuses. Compact condition names and inline effects appear on Year, family, dating, and heir profiles.

Schema 17 stores condition IDs on every person. Existing people in older saves receive empty lists without rolls or stat bonuses. Older reviews retain recorded choices and former limits; new reviews store their maximum and budget.

### Abandonment and care by relatives

`household_parent_abandonment` is an automatic event for a living, present parent while the player starts the year under 18. Its annual roll is `0.00005 + 0.01995 * ((10 - stability) / 10)^2 * ((10 - relationship) / 10)^2`, with both inputs bounded to 0-10. This ranges from 0.005% to 2%; it does not receive the ordinary household multiplier. It shares the five-event annual cap. Selected departures resolve before other household outcomes, and other selected events for a departed parent are skipped. A parent can leave only once; guardians never receive the abandonment event.

Each departure permanently changes player mental stability by base -3, social -1.5, and health -1, with directional rating response. Separate departures stack consequences. The remaining parent loses 3 mental stability and 1 health directly and becomes sole caregiver; normal development/events can later change those ratings. If none remain, an adult aunt or uncle is generated as guardian and supplies support. Settling in permanently changes mental stability by base -1.5 and social -0.5. Old saved timed departure/adoption modifiers expire normally. Parent events and routine care apply to guardians; another relative replaces a deceased guardian while the player is a minor.

Biological parents are preserved in `parents`; `abandonedAt` records the completed age when they left, without marking them dead. `state.guardian` is optional/null or a persisted adult profile with `guardianRole: 'aunt' | 'uncle'` and `adoptedAt`. `caregivers(state)` returns living present parents, or the living guardian if none remain. Departed parents provide no household support and receive no routine family-time/crying effects or household events. Their identities and earlier history remain visible. A sibling succession retains the shared guardian and departure history; child and unrelated-beneficiary successions clear the prior guardian. Preview reads never generate a relative, consume RNG, or invent historical before-values for a new guardian.

## Mortality and annual reviews

[mortality.js](mortality.js) stores exact-age 0–119 death probabilities from the [SSA 2023 period life table in the 2026 Trustees Report](https://www.ssa.gov/oact/STATS/table4c6.html). The equal-weight mean of male/female rates supplies one representative US baseline. The game has no country, medical-sex, or medical-history model; identity and pregnancy capability do not select a mortality table. Beyond the source horizon, the final rate is retained; no birthday guarantees death.

The baseline probability is converted to a hazard and divided between disease/natural causes and accidents. Health scales disease hazard by `exp((5 - health) × 0.14)`. Adult mental stability scales accident hazard by `exp((5 - stability) × 0.035)`; under-18 mental stability never changes accident risk. At health/mental stability 5 the total reproduces the baseline. The competing hazards convert back to one annual death probability, preventing double counting. Stat multipliers and the coarse cause split are game approximations; the split is informed by [NCHS leading causes of death in 2023](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nvsr/nvsr74/nvsr74-010.pdf), not fitted cause-specific rates. These are population-inspired odds, not individual medical predictions.

Deaths store cause/age, appear in history and the year review, stop the person's aging/events, and reduce player mental stability according to the relationship. A remembered partner does not block new dating. Player death enables succession at any age; age 85 still requires a playable handoff without marking a survivor dead.

`annualForecast` clones state and runs mean routine growth, finances, family aging, and effect expiration. Its internal result retains effective-stat, lifestyle, and parent deltas, and infancy/adult non-college UI renders the combined changes as signed labels in the choices box without numerical forecast deltas. Toddler rows isolate each activity instead of showing this complete routine forecast. It never changes live state or consumes RNG. Routine variation, careers, events, deaths, relationships, births, and unresolved choices can alter outcomes. `yearSummary` stores actual changes and journal events; its popup shows before → after values for player stats, height, lifestyle, and parent mental stability/relationship/lifestyle. Optional `decisions` captures `{ cryLevel }` before age 2 or `{ focus, workLabel }` afterward, before advancement. `workLabel: 'Play'` preserves toddler labels across the fifth birthday; historical four-key reviews retain Explore, while five-key reviews show Electronics and Arts & crafts; School/College/Career remain correct across later transitions. Historical age-two cry snapshots stay valid. Reviews show past choices and unused points, never current controls; older summaries omit the section. The popup reopens until dismissed, and later event-choice outcomes are not retroactively added.

## Relationships and children

Single adults can meet zero to five prospective partners in a year. Attractiveness, social, and lifestyle determine encounter opportunities and influence generated profiles and pursuit success; social focus also affects frequency. At default social focus, minimum desirability ratings yield about 0.22 expected encounters per year and at most one, while maximum ratings yield about 4.6 and at most five.

Profiles show skills, affinities, birth conditions, health, mental stability, age, height, and lifestyle. Each encounter can be pursued as a relationship, approached for a consensual one-night stand, or ignored. Both approaches can be declined. An encounter is consumed once; unanswered encounters expire next year. Starting a relationship closes other invitations.

New relationships start at a random relationship of 3–7 out of 10. Dating gives effective social +0.7 and mental stability +0.6; marriage gives +0.9 and +0.8. Family focus develops relationship and partner wellbeing. Proposals are limited to once per year. Relationship and years together determine acceptance; six years together adds 42 percentage points at the same relationship.

Pregnancy base probabilities are 2% yearly while dating, 25% while married, and 1% after an accepted casual encounter, adjusted for the gestating person's age and health. Gestating ages are 18–45. Pregnancy is saved and leads to birth on the next yearly advance. No new pregnancy starts when another exists, when the player is at least 84, or when family capacity is full.

Manual **haveChild** currently means adoption, available from 18 without requiring a partner. There is a six-child cap, an expected baby reserves a place, and only one child arrival is allowed per player age. Children have their own stats, affinities, birth conditions, height, identity, and relationship; family focus supports their development. Parent/partner/child events can affect that person, the player, or both. Ending a relationship preserves children and any expected baby. New children record known `parentIds` at birth/adoption so a recorded co-parent can survive a child handoff; older saves do not infer unknown parentage.

Appearance fluctuates slightly each year around a flat mean until the requested gender-based threshold: men begin mean losses in the year 30→31, women in 22→23, and other genders currently default to 30. Mean decline is `-(0.06 + min(40, age - threshold) * 0.0018)` before annual variation. Specific authored events can still change appearance. This is a requested gameplay rule, not a scientific attractiveness standard. `appearanceDeclineAge` is authoritative; `appearancePeakAge` remains in saves for compatibility. Gender appears beside the name. Identity, attraction, growth profile, and pregnancy capability remain separate; there is no new-game gender-selection screen yet.

## Retirement and inheritance

The engine chooses candidates in this order: **eligible children → eligible siblings → unrelated beneficiary**. Eligible means living, younger than 85, and not a previously played character. New games start with no siblings; siblings become available through earlier child handoffs.

With no eligible family heir, the preview generates one child aged 6–12 with a different surname and the same broad natural-rating distributions as other new people. This preview is deterministic across menu visits and reloads and does not consume gameplay randomness.

The internal estate calculation is:

~~~text
legacy rate = clamp(0.95 - 0.05 × lifestyle, 0.45, 0.90)
estate = floor(money × legacy rate)
child share = estate / number of eligible children
~~~

Every eligible child receives exactly the same fractional share, including children not selected as the next player. With no eligible children, the selected sibling or beneficiary receives the whole estate. Do not display these amounts in the game.

An heir retains their age, stats, affinities, birth conditions, height, identity, and own resources, then receives inheritance. The new playable chapter resets career, partner, children, pregnancy, temporary effects, pending choices, focus, cry level, year summary, and education progress; the new player is alive. Adult heirs choose college/work again. Child/sibling heirs retain the family surname regardless of gender; an unrelated beneficiary begins a different family line. The journal and lineage preserve previous chapters. A child handoff preserves the previous player's life/death status and their current partner only when that partner is a recorded co-parent. A sibling handoff preserves existing parents and remaining siblings.
